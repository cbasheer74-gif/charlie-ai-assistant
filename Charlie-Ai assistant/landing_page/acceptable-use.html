Acceptable Use Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Version 1.0
Core Purpose: CHARLIE is designed to empower creative professionals, engineers, and everyday computer users through ethical, safe local AI automation. This policy defines prohibited activities that violate our service standards.

1. Prohibited Cyber & Security Activities

You may NOT use CHARLIE to:

  • Develop, distribute, or execute malware, trojans, ransomware, spyware, or malicious payloads.
  • Conduct unauthorized vulnerability scans, denial-of-service (DoS) attacks, or automated brute-forcing.
  • Attempt to reverse-engineer, decompile, or tamper with CHARLIE's cryptographic licensing authority or RSA entitlement verification.
  • Forge hardware identifiers or spoof device telemetry to bypass the One-Active-PC policy.

2. Abuse of Automation & Web Scraping

When utilizing CHARLIE's web automation and browser subagents:

  • Do not bypass CAPTCHAs or access-control barriers on third-party services without permission.
  • Do not scrape private personal information, credentials, or protected copyrighted repositories in violation of third-party terms.
  • Comply with rate limits and robots.txt policies of target web destinations.

3. Prohibited Content & Communication

You may not instruct CHARLIE to generate or transmit:

  • Illegal, harmful, fraudulent, or deceptive content.
  • Harassment, threats, non-consensual deepfakes, or hate speech.
  • Unsolicited bulk email (spam) or automated telemarketing campaigns.

4. Enforcement & Account Suspension

Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy may result in immediate suspension or permanent termination of your software license without refund, and where appropriate, referral to law enforcement authorities.

5. Reporting Violations

To report potential abuse of CHARLIE software, contact Security & Abuse Response.
