Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Version 1.0
Zero Marketing Tracking: CHARLIE's website and web portal operate on a strictly functional, privacy-first storage architecture. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral analytics pixels.

1. What Technologies We Use

When you visit the CHARLIE web portal, we utilize modern browser localStorage, sessionStorage, and secure HTTP cookies strictly necessary to provide authentication and manage commercial transactions.

2. Inventory of Storage Items

Key / Cookie Storage Type Purpose Duration
charlie_access_token localStorage Cryptographic JWT token used to authorize API calls against the licensing server. 15 minutes (auto-refreshed)
charlie_refresh_token localStorage Secure refresh credential used to renew expired access sessions without re-login. 30 days
charlie_auth_user localStorage Caches display name, email, and subscription plan for instant portal UI rendering. Persistent until logout
pending_checkout_plan sessionStorage Remembers user's selected tier across authentication redirects during checkout. Current browser tab session

3. Why No Cookie Banner Is Required

Under international privacy regulations (including GDPR Article 5(3) and the ePrivacy Directive), strictly necessary functional storage required to fulfill a user-requested service (such as maintaining an authenticated user account) is exempt from consent opt-in requirements.

4. How to Manage Local Storage

You can clear stored credentials at any time by clicking "Sign Out" in the Customer Portal or by clearing your browser cache and local storage in your browser settings.

5. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding this policy, visit our Customer Support Center.
