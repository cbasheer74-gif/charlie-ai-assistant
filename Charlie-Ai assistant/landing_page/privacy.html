Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Version 1.0 • Anees Chaudhary / CHARLIE AI Assistant
Privacy-First Commitment: CHARLIE is engineered by Anees Chaudhary as a local-first Windows assistant. We collect only the minimum telemetry and metadata required to securely authenticate accounts, verify subscriptions, and deliver updates.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Details: Email address, hashed password (Argon2/bcrypt), display name, and subscription tier.
  • Device Identifiers: Non-reversible SHA-256 hashed hardware identifier used exclusively to enforce the One-Active-PC policy and prevent licensing fraud. Raw hardware serial numbers or MAC addresses are never stored.
  • Payment Records: Transaction IDs, order numbers, amounts, and dates returned by Razorpay. We never see or store debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs.

2. User Content & Local Processing

Your local files, spreadsheets, emails, recordings, and conversations processed on your desktop remain on your computer by default. CHARLIE does not harvest user documents for AI training.

3. Cloud AI & Third-Party Services (BYOK)

When you enable external AI engines (such as Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, or Groq) using your own API keys (Bring Your Own Key), queries sent to those providers are governed by their respective privacy terms. CHARLIE transmits data to cloud APIs only when you explicitly configure and initiate those tasks.

4. Diagnostics & Crash Reporting Policy

In the event of an application error, crash reports are sanitized using automated redaction filters to scrub personal paths, API tokens, and credentials. Users can select their preferred diagnostics reporting policy at any time:

  • Ask Every Time: Prompts for confirmation before sending a diagnostic report.
  • Auto-Send: Automatically dispatches sanitized technical stack traces to assist engineering resolution.
  • Never Send: Suppresses all outbound diagnostic telemetry entirely.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted infrastructure providers strictly necessary to deliver commercial software services:

  • Razorpay: PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway for order creation and payment processing in Indian Rupees (INR).
  • Cloud AI Providers (Optional BYOK): When you configure Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, or Groq API keys, requests route directly to those providers subject to your personal account settings.

6. Data Retention Schedules

  • Account Credentials: Retained for the duration of your active account.
  • Financial Invoices: Payment transaction metadata (order ID, amount, timestamp) is retained as required by statutory tax and accounting regulations.
  • Device Identifiers: Retained while your license is active on that machine; cleared upon license deactivation or device transfer.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Telemetry: Sanitized error traces are automatically purged after 90 days.

7. Data Subject Rights & Account Deletion

Under applicable global privacy principles, you have the right to:

  • Access & Inspect: View your active profile, bound hardware, subscription tier, and payment history at any time via the Customer Portal.
  • Rectification: Update your display name and password in your account settings.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You may request full account deletion through the Customer Portal or by submitting a ticket via our Support Center. Upon account deletion, all active credentials, license keys, and device hashes are permanently erased.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website utilizes strictly essential local storage for authentication. Review our dedicated Cookie & Storage Policy for full details.
