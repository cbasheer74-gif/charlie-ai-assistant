Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Version 1.0-draft
Transparent Terms: We provide free 10-minute daily access under our Starter tier so all customers can thoroughly evaluate CHARLIE on their Windows hardware before purchasing any paid plan.

1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your recurring subscription (Basic ₹99/mo, Premium ₹199/mo, Advanced ₹299/mo) at any time directly through the Customer Account Portal. No phone calls or retention dark patterns are required.

  • Cancellation takes effect immediately for the subsequent billing cycle.
  • Your features will remain active until the end of the paid 30-day period.
  • Upon expiration, your account seamlessly transitions back to the free Starter tier with no data loss.

2. Refund Eligibility & Request Window

Because CHARLIE offers a persistent free trial and digital software activation upon payment confirmation, refund requests are evaluated according to the following guidelines:

  • Initial Purchase Window: Customers who encounter persistent hardware incompatibility on Windows 10/11 that our technical support team cannot resolve may request a full refund within 7 calendar days of their initial payment.
  • Lifetime Licenses: Due to permanent cryptographic entitlement issuance, Lifetime purchases (₹999) are refundable within 48 hours of initial order if no activation or usage was logged, or within 7 days in case of verified unresolvable hardware failure.
  • Renewals: Recurring monthly renewal charges are non-refundable once processed. Please cancel prior to your renewal date in your Customer Portal.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, submit a ticket via our Support Center or email billing support with your order ID, account email, and reason for cancellation.
