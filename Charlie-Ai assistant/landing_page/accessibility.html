Accessibility Statement

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Version 1.0
Commitment to Accessible Computing: We are committed to making the CHARLIE web portal, documentation, and desktop assistant accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of ability or assistive technology.

1. Design Standards & Principles

Our web platform and desktop UI are designed with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 principles in mind:

  • Color Contrast: Text and critical interactive elements maintain high contrast against dark backgrounds (minimum 4.5:1 ratio for normal text).
  • Keyboard Navigation: Interactive elements (modals, forms, tables, search toolbars) support full keyboard access via Tab, Enter, Space, and Escape.
  • Visual Focus Indicators: Focused controls exhibit clear visual focus rings for keyboard users.
  • Semantic HTML: Use of standard landmarks (header, nav, main, footer) and ARIA attributes for screen readers.
  • Voice-First Modality: CHARLIE Desktop provides full speech-to-text input and natural text-to-speech output, allowing hands-free computing.

2. Ongoing Improvements

While we continuously audit and improve accessibility across Windows high-contrast modes and screen readers (such as Windows Narrator and NVDA), certain complex visual graphs or terminal logs may still present accessibility barriers. We actively prioritize accessibility fixes in our engineering roadmap.

3. Feedback & Contact

If you experience any accessibility barrier or have suggestions for improvements, please reach out to our team via the Customer Support Center. We welcome your feedback and aim to respond within 2 business days.
