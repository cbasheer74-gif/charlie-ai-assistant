Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Version 1.0-draft
Privacy-First Commitment: CHARLIE is designed as a local-first Windows assistant. We collect only the minimum telemetry and metadata required to securely authenticate accounts, verify subscriptions, and deliver updates.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Details: Email address, hashed password (Argon2/bcrypt), display name, and subscription tier.
  • Device Identifiers: Non-reversible SHA-256 hashed hardware identifier used exclusively to enforce the One-Active-PC policy and prevent licensing fraud. Raw hardware serial numbers or MAC addresses are never stored.
  • Payment Records: Transaction IDs, order numbers, amounts, and dates returned by Razorpay. We never see or store debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs.

2. User Content & Local Processing

Your local files, spreadsheets, emails, recordings, and conversations processed on your desktop remain on your computer by default. CHARLIE does not harvest user documents for AI training.

3. Cloud AI & Third-Party Services (BYOK)

When you enable external AI engines (such as Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, or Groq) using your own API keys (Bring Your Own Key), queries sent to those providers are governed by their respective privacy terms. CHARLIE transmits data to cloud APIs only when you explicitly configure and initiate those tasks.

4. Diagnostics & Crash Reporting Policy

In the event of an application error, crash reports are sanitized using automated redaction filters to scrub personal paths, API tokens, and credentials. Users can select their preferred diagnostics reporting policy at any time:

  • Ask Every Time: Prompts for confirmation before sending a diagnostic report.
  • Auto-Send: Automatically dispatches sanitized technical stack traces to assist engineering resolution.
  • Never Send: Suppresses all outbound diagnostic telemetry entirely.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our public website uses strictly essential session cookies to maintain authenticated login states. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers or sell personal data.
