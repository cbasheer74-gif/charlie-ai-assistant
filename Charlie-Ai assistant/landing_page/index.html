Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about licensing, safety, and system capabilities.

How does the 10-minute daily Starter plan work? +
Starter gives you 10 active minutes of real assistant interaction every day (speaking, processing tasks, and automating workflows). Idle desktop time does not deplete your timer. Once the 10 active minutes conclude, CHARLIE pauses interactive commands and invites you to subscribe or return tomorrow when the allowance resets.
What happens if my subscription expires? Are my files or memories deleted? +
Never. Your personal memories, Knowledge Graph, created files, and conversation history are 100% stored on your local computer and remain completely intact. An expired plan simply returns to the free Starter tier without any data loss.
How does the Lifetime ₹999 license work? +
The Lifetime license gives you permanent access to Advanced application capabilities—including autonomous PC control, Filmora 14 video editing, dual voices, and custom plugins—with no recurring monthly subscription fee ever. It includes RSA-2048 signed offline licensing for disconnected use. One active PC at a time — transfer your license to a new computer instantly via your account login.
Can CHARLIE work without an internet connection? +
Yes. CHARLIE includes local offline AI routing using on-device quantized models and local speech synthesis. While cloud connectivity enables web search and cloud vision models, core desktop control, memory, and automation work offline seamlessly.
How does Filmora 14 video automation work? +
CHARLIE interfaces directly with Wondershare Filmora 14's COM/automation pipeline and FFmpeg. You can instruct CHARLIE to import video clips, slice silences, apply LUTs, generate synchronized subtitle tracks, and render vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts or TikToks automatically.
